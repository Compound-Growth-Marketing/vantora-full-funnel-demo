Which paid sources drive pipeline?

Spend and platform leads pull daily from Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, LinkedIn, Meta and StackAdapt. CRM leads, MQLs, SALs, opportunities and pipeline come from the CRM, matched to campaign by UTM and click ID.
Monthly targets load from the approved quarterly media plan. The same template connects to each client's ad accounts and CRM, so a new client view is configured, not rebuilt.
Leads, MQLs and spend follow the date filter. Pacing is month-to-date; opportunities and pipeline are quarter-to-date.
